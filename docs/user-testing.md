# User testing: v1 (Nov 17)

Who: the three people interviewed for the proposal, or other UChicago students who use dating apps.
How: in person or on a call with screen share. **Give them the task, then watch without helping.**
Each session takes about 10 minutes.

## The task (read this out, then stop talking)

> "Here's a link. Pretend you just matched with someone and the chat is going nowhere.
> Use this to see how a first date with them might go. Think out loud as you go."

Send them the live link. Let them pick a demo person, or, if two testers are together, have one
invite the other.

## What to watch for (write down what they DO and SAY, not what you think)

| Moment | Watch for |
|---|---|
| Landing page | Do they get what it is in 10 seconds? What do they say first? |
| Interview | Which question makes them pause or skip? Do they write like they text? How long does it take? |
| "Does this sound like you?" | Do they agree? What feels wrong? (fidelity) |
| Choosing | Demo person or invite? Do they understand the invite? |
| Live date | Do they read it or skim? Do they laugh, cringe, say "that's so me" / "I'd never say that"? |
| Report | Which part do they read most closely? Does "Worth talking about" feel fair or harsh? |
| Want to meet? | Would they really tap yes with a real match? Why or why not? |

## After the task, ask only these

1. "What was that, in your own words?"
2. "Would you send this link to a real match? What would stop you?"
3. "Was there a moment your clone didn't sound like you?"
